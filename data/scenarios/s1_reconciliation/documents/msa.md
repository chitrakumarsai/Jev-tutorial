# Master Services Agreement — Freight Transportation

**Agreement No.** NWL-MSA-2026-014
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Parties:** Northwind Logistics LLC ("Carrier") and Harbor & Pine Retail Inc. ("Customer")

> Synthetic document created for the Jev Audit Lens demo. All names, figures and terms are fictional.

## 1. Term

1.1 This Agreement runs for one contract year, from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026 (the "Contract Year").

1.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 90 days' written notice.

## 2. Services

2.1 Carrier provides full-truckload line-haul transport from Customer's distribution centre in Zone A (regional, under 250 miles) and Zone B (long haul, 250 miles or more).

2.2 Each completed delivery of one trailer to one destination is one "Load".

## 3. Rates

3.1 Line-haul rates. Zone A: $1,250.00 per Load. Zone B: $1,480.00 per Load.

3.2 Detention. Waiting time beyond two free hours at a Customer site is billed at $85.00 per hour, per occurrence, as a single line.

3.3 Onboarding fee. A one-time onboarding fee of $500.00 was invoiced and paid on signature and is not billed again.

## 4. Volume discount

4.1 When cumulative Loads in the Contract Year exceed 1,000, Customer receives a discount of 5% off all line-haul charges.

4.2 The discount applies to every invoice issued for the calendar month after the month in which cumulative Loads first exceed 1,000, and to all later invoices in the Contract Year.

## 5. Fuel surcharge

5.1 Carrier may add a fuel surcharge calculated as a percentage of the line-haul charges billed on the same invoice (after any volume discount).

5.2 The surcharge percentage follows the published diesel index for the invoice month but may not exceed 18% of line-haul charges.

## 6. Invoicing and payment

6.1 Carrier invoices monthly, on the last day of the month, for Loads completed in that month. Each invoice states the Loads per zone, the line-haul subtotal, the fuel surcharge percentage and any detention.

6.2 Payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date.

6.3 Late fee. If an invoice is paid more than 30 days after its invoice date, Carrier may charge a late fee of 1.5% of that invoice's balance, billed on a later invoice.

## 7. Insurance and liability

7.1 Carrier maintains cargo liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence.

7.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.

## 8. Audit rights

8.1 Customer may audit Carrier's invoices and supporting records once per Contract Year with 15 business days' notice. Overbilled amounts found in an audit are credited within 30 days.

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
