# Data Processing and Services Addendum

**Addendum No.:** NWL-DPSA-2026-003
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Parties:** Northwind Logistics LLC ("Carrier") and Harbor & Pine Retail Inc. ("Customer")
**Supplements:** Master Services Agreement NWL-MSA-2026-014 (the "MSA")

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Addendum sets out additional terms for the shipment-tracking portal and the
handling of Customer shipment data by Carrier.
1.2 Where this Addendum conflicts with the MSA, this Addendum prevails for the subject
matter it covers.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Customer Data" means consignee names, delivery addresses, contact numbers and
shipment contents that Customer provides to Carrier.
2.2 "Portal" means Carrier's web-based shipment-tracking service.
2.3 "Fees" means the amounts invoiced to Customer under the MSA.

## 3. Processing of Customer Data

3.1 Carrier will process Customer Data only to plan, perform and track shipments.
3.2 Carrier will keep Customer Data confidential and restrict access to staff who need it.
3.3 Carrier will apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including
encryption in transit and role-based access to the Portal.

## 4. Subcontractors

4.1 Carrier may engage subcontractors to perform line-haul or last-mile services
without Customer's prior consent.
4.2 Carrier will tell Customer about each new subcontractor within 30 days of engaging it.
4.3 Carrier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if
they were its own.

## 5. Audit

5.1 If Customer disputes an invoice, Customer may ask Carrier for the delivery records
supporting the disputed line items.
5.2 Carrier will provide those delivery records within 15 business days of the request.
5.3 Undisputed amounts remain payable on the due date.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

6.1 Carrier will retain Customer Data only for as long as the MSA is in force.
6.2 Within 30 days after the MSA ends, Carrier will delete all Customer Data and, on
written request, certify the deletion in writing.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 Carrier will maintain the insurance required by Section 9 of the MSA throughout
the term of this Addendum.

## 8. Indemnity

8.1 Each party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims that its
software or materials infringe intellectual property rights.
8.2 The indemnity in 8.1 does not apply to claims caused by the indemnified party's own
modifications or misuse.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential losses.
9.2 Each party's total liability under this Addendum is limited to the Fees paid in the
one (1) month immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 10. Term, Renewal and Termination

10.1 This Addendum runs for an initial term of twelve (12) months from the effective date.
10.2 It renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party
gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the
current term.
10.3 Either party may terminate this Addendum for convenience on sixty (60) days'
written notice to the other party.
10.4 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party commits a material
breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice.
